Most radio apps want your email first, then your habits, and only then will they let you listen. SonoRadio flips that around: open the app, pick a station, and it plays on your Sonos. Done.

No account. No cloud. No ads between the songs.

SonoRadio talks directly to the Sonos speakers on your own network. Nothing travels to a server of ours, because there isn't one. What you listen to stays between you and your speaker.

WHAT YOU GET

- 144 radio stations, neatly grouped by country: the Netherlands, Belgium, the UK, Germany and an international selection.
- A country picker that stays tidy, even as more countries are added later. Your favourites and what you just played sit right at the top.
- Search that runs across every station at once, with the flag shown so you never confuse Dutch Qmusic with the Belgian one.
- An alarm that wakes your Sonos with the station you choose, at the time you set.
- Missing a station? Paste a stream URL and it's there.

WHO IT'S FOR

For anyone with one or more Sonos speakers who simply wants to listen to radio, without a music-service account, without a voice assistant, without a profile being built about you.

HOW IT WORKS

SonoRadio finds your Sonos speakers on your wifi by itself. Pick the room, pick the station, set the volume. Got several speakers? Switch between them at the top.

PRIVACY

SonoRadio collects nothing, sends nothing, and has no account. All settings stay on your device. The app works within your own network.

SonoRadio is an independent app and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Sonos, Inc.
